# Forktown — Design Context

## Target audience
Platform/engineering leads and AI-agent builders who rehearse dangerous migrations (billing, auth, DB). They want to *feel* the town under pressure—not stare at another SaaS dashboard.

## Use cases
1. Found a living town around a real repo
2. Walk the map, talk to subjective minds, start a rehearsal
3. Watch the world fight the change (buyers, tickets, outages, legacy bugs)
4. Read a survival report and decide whether to ship

## Brand personality
Cozy · playful · earnest. Stardew Valley energy for serious engineering. Warm soil, gold coins, chunky voxels. Friendly without being childish. Never neon AI, never glassmorphism SaaS.

## Aesthetic direction
**Retro 8-bit voxel farm-town** — Press Start 2P titles, VT323 body, chunky pixel panels with hard shadows, grass/sky/soil palette, isometric-ish voxel buildings, CRT scanlines, day/night sky cycles. The living pixel town *is* the product UI.

## Differentiation
You open Forktown and you’re already in a tiny world—agents are villagers, districts are barns and piers, rehearsals feel like quests. Not a chat UI. Not an eval leaderboard.
